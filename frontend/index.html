The Jacaranda City

Over 65,000 jacarandas, one city.

Pretoria's first two jacaranda trees arrived in 1888. In 1906, J.D. Clark donated 200 more for the city's 51st anniversary, and the streets have turned purple every October and November since.

Jacaranda City brings that same sense of community online: one place to see what's on, hear from the city and get things fixed.
